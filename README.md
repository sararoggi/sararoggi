##  🔮 🕯️ Projects 🕯️ 🔮

☾ **[ BEAST ](https://github.com/hexWitches/beast)** - An RDF/OWL ontology for representing real and imaginary creatures, connecting their textual, morphological, etymological, and symbolic dimensions.

☾ **[ Here to Stay ](https://github.com/metamuses/here-to-stay)** - A Linked Open Data project exploring touristification and housing availability in Bologna, combining data analysis with visualizations to investigate its social impact.

☾ **[ LODissea ](https://github.com/hexWitches/LODissea)** - An exploratory study of Europeana’s open data ecosystem, examining its providers, institutional categories, and the structural quality of its metadata.

☾ **[ Salem Witch Trials ](https://github.com/hexWitches/salemWitchTrials)** - A digital humanities project exploring the Salem Witch Trials through archival and cultural materials, with a focus on how historical memory is constructed and transmitted.

☾ **[ The SMT Library ](https://github.com/hexWitches/semantic-tarot-library)** - A semantic digital library exploring the history of Tarot through Linked Open Data. It connects cards, decks, creators, and symbolic motifs into an interconnected environment.

☾ **[ Treasures of Dozza ](https://github.com/boloICOMs/TreasuresofDozza)** - A playful educational application designed for children visiting Dozza. Through small games and challenges, it encourages them to explore the town and discover its murals.

☾ **[ Understory ](https://github.com/sararoggi/understory)** - An NLP project exploring how nature acts in Romantic-era English literature. A custom-trained NER model identifies non-human natural entities, while dependency analysis examines their grammatical roles.

☾ **[Witches Through Time ](https://github.com/hexWitches/WitchesThroughTime)** - A digital exhibition exploring the history of witchcraft through a feminist lens. It brings together artworks, books, historical documents, and contemporary installations within a curatorial narrative.
